# HYPOTHESIS-PRODUCT-002 — ACTION / HISTORY CYCLE

State: `NOT TESTED EXTERNALLY`
Class: `PRODUCT HYPOTHESIS`
Date: 2026-08-23

## Primary hypothesis

Different people may enter Célula Zero through different immediate needs, while
value emerges if those entries converge into a shared cycle:

```text
intention
-> discovery
-> relation
-> action/commitment
-> contribution/result
-> contextual history
-> future opportunity
```

A single universal onboarding action is not required by this hypothesis.

## H1 — Shared History Bootstrap

### Claim

Reconstructed shared history can reduce cold start of context and relations
without pretending that Célula Zero observed the past.

### Mechanism candidate

- self-claim;
- artifact/reference;
- external source/signal;
- attributed claim from another participant;
- explicit basis of knowledge when useful.

### Counter-evidence / risk

The invite may be only a social favor and produce no value for the invited
person or no return behavior.

### Cheapest falsifying test

Invite a real co-participant to inspect/respond to one real past occurrence
without requiring account creation first.

### Local failure criterion / negative evidence

The invited person provides a low-information courtesy response and gains no
reason to use the resulting context for themselves or any later action.

## H2 — Present Action Utility

### Claim

A real Project/Need/Opportunity can turn an intention into a relation and a
real-world action while preserving the conditions and context that produced it.

### Mechanism candidate

```text
Need / Opportunity
-> Interest / Proposal
-> Human decision
-> Commitment
-> Action
-> Contribution / Result
```

### Counter-evidence / risk

The flow may add no value beyond a referral, existing marketplace or WhatsApp
conversation, creating a project/opportunity graveyard.

### Cheapest falsifying test

Use one real need with one real potential participant and observe whether the CZ
representation changes discovery, agreement, execution or later history.

### Local failure criterion / negative evidence

The relationship proceeds exactly as it would have without CZ and no preserved
context is later useful to either participant.

## H3 — Live World Discovery

### Claim

A person can obtain value before constructing a rich profile by seeing a small
set of real, relevant things they can act on.

### Counter-evidence / risk

Without enough real supply the surface becomes empty; with broad supply it
becomes noisy.

### Cheapest falsifying test

Expose one external participant to a curated but entirely real set of available
actions in a specific context.

### Local failure criterion / negative evidence

The participant cannot find anything they want to inspect/act on without human
explanation or artificial content.

## H4 — Profile Projection

### Claim

A useful profile can emerge progressively from relationships, actions, shared
history and external signals instead of requiring a large manual form.

### Counter-evidence / risk

Derived history may be too sparse or ambiguous to communicate what the person
needs others to know.

### Cheapest falsifying test

After a real action, show the resulting projected profile/context to the person
and to a relevant third-party decision maker.

### Local failure criterion / negative evidence

Both still need a manually curated CV/portfolio/profile to understand the
relevant capability/context, and the CZ projection adds no decision value.

## H5 — Native History Advantage

### Claim

History produced during real CZ-mediated activity can preserve more useful
provenance than retrospective profile editing.

### Required distinction

`PREPARED != COMMITTED != EXECUTED != VERIFIED != OUTCOME`.

### Local failure criterion / negative evidence

The extra native context never changes a later decision, explanation or ability
to reconstruct what happened.

## H6 — Multiple Entry Points

### Claim

Different participant types can enter through different immediate jobs while
converging on the same underlying action/history cycle.

Potential examples:

- learner: `I want to learn/do`;
- organizer: `I need someone`;
- provider: `I can help/offer`;
- experienced participant: `something I did before matters`;
- offline worker: `someone I worked with contextualized me`.

### Local failure criterion / negative evidence

The different entry points create incompatible journeys that do not converge on
shared objects/relations or require separate products.

## Evidence policy

No numerical threshold in this document is a market benchmark unless externally
sourced and explicitly cited. Experimental thresholds, if introduced, are
pre-registered decision aids only.

A local failure in one participant/context is negative evidence for that run. It
does not, by itself, falsify a general human-behavior or market hypothesis.
`FAIL N=1 != UNIVERSAL HYPOTHESIS FALSIFIED`.
