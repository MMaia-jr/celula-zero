## Future human participants

Future human participants must be identified according to the provenance requirements of the project.

Where appropriate, the record should distinguish:

- author of an original contribution;
- interviewee;
- researcher;
- reviewer;
- translator;
- curator;
- organizer;
- witness;
- source;
- person or community represented in the material.

Participation must not erase individual attribution.

## Communities and collective knowledge

A community must not automatically be represented as the “author” or “owner” of material merely because knowledge is associated with that community.

Questions of collective authority, representation, consent, custody and disclosure must be recorded separately and, whenever possible, determined with the people concerned rather than inferred by the repository maintainers.
