# Protocol

Canonicality is determined by repository history and human-authorized promotion. This file expresses durable operating rules; **`STATE.md` determines the current milestone and next gate**.

## 1. Foundational principles preserved from the earlier protocol

The following principles are carried forward in their prior wording rather than silently rewritten:

1. Proveniência antes de consenso.
2. Interpretação não substitui autoria.
3. Descentralização decisória sem simetria informacional é descentralização aparente.
4. Simetria informacional sem capacidade de verificação é transparência aparente.
5. Nenhuma síntese constitui decisão coletiva automaticamente.
6. Mudança de posição é informação.
7. A própria Célula Zero é também objeto de estudo.

These principles remain compatible with the current Operating Loop. Their presence does not mean every historical experiment remains on the current critical path.

## 2. Mission and method

Mission:

`intention → learning → production → evidence → evaluation → capability → contextual trust → opportunity`

Principle:

`learn by building`

Prefer:

`real problem/intention → discover what must be learned → smallest real action → observable production → feedback → reflection → record → next cycle`

## 3. Epistemic invariants

Preserve:

`Original Record ≠ Interpretation ≠ Claim ≠ Evidence ≠ Verification ≠ Decision ≠ Reputation`

`activity ≠ contribution ≠ result ≠ evidence ≠ evaluation ≠ reputation`

Do not automatically transform:

- source → truth;
- provenance → trust;
- record → evidence;
- evidence → proof;
- AI consensus → legitimacy;
- activity → value;
- visibility → reputation.

A legitimate human direction is a `DECISION / HUMAN DIRECTION`. Its consequences remain testable.

Preserve:

`PASS / FAIL / PARTIAL / INCONCLUSIVE`

## 4. Current Operating Loop

The internally demonstrated loop is:

`Opportunity → Proposal → Commitment → Contribution → Artifact → Claim/Evidence → Verification`

Operational meanings:

- **Opportunity** — a bounded reason to act under explicit context and conditions.
- **Proposal** — an attributed offer to perform work under stated conditions.
- **Commitment** — explicit acceptance of a Proposal by an authorized counterparty.
- **Contribution** — work or resource actually provided in the relevant context.
- **Artifact** — an observable output or reference to output.
- **Claim** — an attributed, contestable statement about a contribution or artifact.
- **Evidence** — an explicit contextual use of a source in relation to a Claim.
- **Verification** — an attributed review under declared criteria and method.

Artifact is not automatically Evidence.
Evidence is not automatically proof.
Verification is not Outcome, Decision or Reputation.

## 5. Human authority

The human founder/steward decides, when applicable:

- vision and values;
- priorities;
- consent;
- human relationships;
- sensitive disclosure;
- final authorizations;
- promotion steps that require explicit authority.

AI agents may:

- research;
- critique;
- synthesize;
- propose;
- produce artifacts;
- review;
- execute within an authorized scope.

AI agents do not silently:

- redefine human intent;
- create legitimate human consent;
- expand their own authority;
- acquire economic rights;
- turn their own evaluation into binding legitimacy.

Voting or consensus among AIs does not substitute for legitimate human authority.

## 6. Execution and promotion

Preserve:

`PREPARED ≠ EXECUTED ≠ VERIFIED ≠ COMMITTED ≠ PUSHED ≠ MERGED ≠ CANONICAL`

A prepared plan is not execution.
Execution is not verification.
Local verification is not canonicalization.

Commit, push, pull request and merge require explicit human authorization unless a prior human decision clearly authorized the full sequence.

The accepted execution-continuity MVP boundary is:

`one human command → bounded self-contained verifiable executor`

Further automation requires a demonstrated concrete property that would otherwise be lost.

## 7. Construction discipline

Prefer:

`criterion → smallest implementation → test → result → reflection → next change`

Use vertical slices.

Before building custom technology, ask:

> Can this property already be preserved by a standard, process or existing infrastructure?

Classify:

`ADOPT / MAP / EXTEND / MISSING`

For `EXTEND` or `MISSING`, ask:

> What concrete property will be lost if we do not build this?

Without a clear answer, do not build.

Do not assume blockchain, DID, VC, IPFS, DAO, token, graph database, RAG, MCP, CLI or a custom platform.

Cloning something to study it does not make it the chosen architecture.

## 8. External utility and habitability

The external-utility question is:

> Does a real person who was not involved in building Célula Zero receive measurable benefit?

The current habitability observation chain is:

`ENTRY → ACTION → RELATION → SECOND PERSON → REAL-WORLD CONSEQUENCE → RETURN`

Each transition must be observed separately.

Views, clicks, signups, likes, declarations of interest or a message sent do not by themselves prove a real-world consequence.

Internal `PASS N=1` does not prove external utility, adoption, PMF or scale.

## 9. Trust and economics

Trust remains contextual:

> I trust X for Y based on Z.

Do not create a universal reputation score in the MVP.

Preserve:

`sponsorship ≠ endorsement ≠ contribution ≠ economic right`

Economic conditions must be prospective and explicit:

`Opportunity → Conditions → Agreement → Contribution`

Participation alone creates no retroactive economic right.

## 10. Security, privacy and resilience

Default to:

- explicit workspace;
- writes only inside authorized scope;
- Git branches/worktrees for changes;
- secrets outside the repository;
- least privilege;
- minimal network access;
- explicit promotion authority;
- LGPD and applicable Brazilian law;
- exportable essential data;
- no SaaS as the only copy of essential records.

Do not place unnecessary personal, confidential, security-sensitive, Indigenous or restricted knowledge in the public repository.

For untrusted repositories, prefer isolated/sandboxed execution and do not expose broad personal credentials or unrelated mounts.

## 11. Repository authority hierarchy

The repository contains both current state and preserved history. Do not treat every artifact as equally authoritative for the present.

Read in this order:

1. `README.md` — front door and orientation.
2. `STATE.md` — current operational state and next gate.
3. active human decisions referenced by `STATE.md`.
4. `PROTOCOL.md` — durable operating rules.
5. active Work Packet / test referenced by the current state.
6. implementation, Result Packages, Claims, Evidence and Verification required for the question.
7. historical rounds, old issues, superseded decisions, old branches and archived experiments.

An old artifact may be valid history without being current direction.

Git history preserves superseded states; `STATE.md` keeps the operational present short.

## 12. Provenance and content classes

Preserve authorship of statements, questions, interpretations and decisions.

Content classes remain distinct:

- **Original Record** — preserved contribution in its original form and context.
- **Interpretation** — attributed reading of another record.
- **Synthesis** — explicit compression or organization.
- **Claim** — attributed contestable assertion.
- **Evidence** — contextual relationship between a source and a Claim.
- **Verification** — attributed evaluation under criteria/method.
- **Decision** — explicit authorized direction or conclusion.
- **Operational Convention** — provisional rule for operating the system.

No class silently replaces another.

Historical gaps must not be reconstructed by inference and presented as Original Records.

## 13. Protocol of Protocols

`genesis/INTENT-000.md` records a longer-term interoperability direction in which different agents or systems may cooperate without adopting one universal internal governance model.

This is a direction under investigation.

It is **not** evidence that a universal protocol is implemented, externally useful, adopted or scalable.
