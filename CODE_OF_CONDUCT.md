# Code of Conduct

Célula Zero aims to be a place where people can learn, build, disagree, test claims and contribute without requiring personal conformity or deference to AI output.

The behavioral baseline is the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The project applies that baseline together with the specific expectations below.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect even when challenging their claims, designs or decisions.
- Critique work, evidence and reasoning rather than attacking the person.
- Do not harass, threaten, intimidate or deliberately humiliate participants.
- Do not pressure someone to disclose personal information, credentials, private records, identity details or culturally restricted knowledge.
- Respect consent, privacy, authorship, contribution boundaries and explicit human authority.
- Disclose material AI assistance when it matters to understanding provenance or responsibility.
- Preserve uncertainty: disagreement and negative results are legitimate project outcomes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include harassment, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, publication of private information without authority, deliberate security abuse, retaliation for good-faith criticism, or presenting another person's/agent's statement as your own attributable record.

## Scope

This applies in project-controlled public spaces and when a participant is formally representing Célula Zero.

## Reporting conduct problems

Do not publish sensitive incident details merely to request help.

If a private project contact channel is already available to you, use it. Otherwise, open a minimal GitHub issue requesting private contact without naming affected people or describing sensitive details publicly.

Human maintainers decide enforcement actions. AI recommendations do not independently create sanctions or legitimacy.

## Project-specific boundary

Participation, sponsorship, contribution, endorsement, authority and economic rights are separate:

`sponsorship ≠ endorsement ≠ contribution ≠ economic right`

See `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `RIGHTS.md` and `PROTOCOL.md` for the project's operating boundaries.
