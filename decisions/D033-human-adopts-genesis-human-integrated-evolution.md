## 5. Integrated shape

Adopt this conceptual integration direction:

```text
Living Presence / Social
         ↕
Interoperability Fabric
         ↕
Spaces / Projects
         ↕
Existing CZ Operating Core
         ↕
Real-world / external rails
```

This is not a requirement to implement five new technical layers.

Its purpose is to prevent false separation between a social experience and the
operational capabilities already built.

The social surface should help Humans discover meaningful intersections and
move into real coordination when they choose.

The existing operating core should preserve the consequential work once a
relation becomes a Project, Agreement or other legitimate operational context.
