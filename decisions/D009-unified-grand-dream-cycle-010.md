## Decision

Célula Zero has one governing evolution process:

`DREAM → PLAN → DO → CELEBRATE`

All projects, experiments, technologies, tranches, opportunities and bounded
learning cycles occur inside that process. They are not parallel operating
systems for Célula Zero.

## Current direction — Capacity First

The current direction is to transform available intelligence, knowledge, tools,
relationships and resources into cumulative increases in participant capacity.

Progress should become observable as consequential change, not merely as:

- more infrastructure;
- more documents;
- more agents;
- more tests;
- more automation;
- more completed internal cycles.

Célula Zero should grow without making its participants smaller.

Economy remains structural.

Sovereignty and progressive decentralization remain part of the Grand Dream.

Web3 remains a legitimate experimental domain, but blockchain, token, DAO,
smart contract, DeFi or any specific technology is not preselected as universal
architecture.

## Grand Dream preserved

Capacity First does not replace, reduce or shrink the Grand Dream.

The longer-horizon ambition remains that Célula Zero may grow very large while
using data, intelligence, infrastructure, capital and coordination — under
consent, explicit rules and legitimate authority — to increase the capacities
of participating beings and organizations rather than centralizing the benefit
of scale only in the center.

Preserve the direction:

> The larger Célula Zero becomes, the larger the capacity of the beings and
> organizations participating in it should be able to become.

Humans, AIs, machines and organizations may develop capacities appropriate to
their different natures and roles without being collapsed into one identical
agent type.

Economic value and resources may be distributed contextually through mechanisms
such as:

- contracts;
- payments;
- grants;
- bounties;
- revenue sharing;
- royalties;
- ventures;
- investments;
- shared infrastructure;
- other explicit agreements justified by real conditions.

No universal rule is adopted that automatically transforms:

`contribution → payment`

`contribution → property`

`contribution → governance power`

`contribution → reputation`

`contribution → permanent authority`

Economic consequence depends on context, evaluation, explicit conditions,
agreement and legitimate authority.

Progressive decentralization remains part of the Grand Dream, but
decentralization does not imply a preselected blockchain, token, DAO or other
technical architecture.

## One human is a legitimate early condition

The absence of a second human does not block evolution of Célula Zero.

External people and real-world episodes remain legitimate and important when
they can produce useful evidence, consequence or capacity, but no universal rule
requires a second human before Célula Zero can learn or evolve.

Likewise:

`multiple AIs ≠ automatically better than one capable AI`.

Agent/model selection should follow the concrete property and task.

## Relationship to previous structures

Previous directions, roadmaps, tracks and experiments remain legitimate history.

They are not erased.

However:

`PRESERVED ≠ CURRENT`

`SUPERSEDED ≠ ERASED`

When a later legitimate Human Direction or Decision changes the operational
path — including when that Decision is informed by new material evidence — the
previous structure remains reconstructible as history or capability but ceases
to govern the present unless explicitly reactivated on a current basis.

Evidence may challenge or inform direction; Evidence does not itself create
legitimate supersession.

Accordingly:

- D006 remains historical Human Direction;
- D007 remains historical sequencing and learning;
- D008 remains historical direction and coordination research;
- Track A / Track B cease to be parallel governing tracks;
- ROADMAP-002 ceases to be the master current gate system;
- INTEGRATED-ALPHA T1/T2/T3 remain valuable canonical capabilities;
- T1→T2→T3→T4 ceases to be the master roadmap;
- AUTONOMOUS-108 Cycles 001–009 remain history, evidence and extracted
  capability rather than an independent active program.
