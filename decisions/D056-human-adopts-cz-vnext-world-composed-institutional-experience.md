## Scope of supersession

D056 supersedes only the part of D052/D055 that made a completely new,
CZ-owned visual product surface the default. It does not erase the need for a
coherent human experience, nor does it accept the current custom interface as
the target merely because it exists.

Preserve from D052/D055:

- Person / Profile / Cell / Authority distinctions;
- attributable provenance and durable consequence;
- continuity across sessions and replaceable modules/capabilities;
- external capabilities that remain replaceable and do not acquire CZ authority;
- Essenthius as institutional intelligence, distinct from model, executor and
  human authority;
- progressive disclosure and reduced human cognitive load;
- Célula Zero using CZ to operate, develop and govern CZ from within its lived
  institutional context.
