# D007 — PRODUCT DISCOVERY TO HABITABLE ALPHA

Class: `DECISION / HUMAN PRODUCT DIRECTION`
Date: 2026-08-23
State: `PREPARED / NOT CANONICAL`

## Original Record

Human direction preserved literally:

> "eae, fizemos muitas investigaçoes , agora esta na hora de avançar ... salvar as investigaçoes e hipoteses e seguir em frente. como vc ve isto acontecendo ?"

Subsequent human authorization to proceed with the proposed transition:

> "ok, vamos . Note que eu nao tenho os arquivos das outras IAs ... vamos seguir sem eles mesmo"

The Original Record is preserved separately from the interpretation below.

## Interpretation

The founder directs the project to stop expanding broad product discovery for
now, preserve the recoverable findings/hypotheses without pretending missing
external-agent files exist, and advance to a small Habitable Alpha experiment
with a real external user.

## Decision

Proceed from broad product discovery to preparation of a narrowly scoped
external-user experiment.

The immediate sequence is:

```text
PRESERVE recoverable discovery
-> SYNTHESIZE surviving findings
-> REGISTER explicit product hypotheses
-> PREPARE HABITABLE-ALPHA-001
-> obtain explicit implementation authority
-> execute smallest required vertical slice
-> run with a real external participant
-> record result
-> human promotion/revision decision
```

## Relationship to D006

`decisions/D006-mvp-habitavel-30-dias.md` is not silently revoked.

D007 narrows the immediate evidence-producing priority: before expanding the
full D006 product scope, the project should test whether a real external person
can obtain utility from a small habitable slice.

Therefore items such as financing rooms, smart-contract demonstrations,
marketplace expansion, native messaging or other broad product capabilities do
not enter the immediate critical path unless HABITABLE-ALPHA-001 demonstrates a
concrete property requiring them.

This is a prioritization/narrowing decision, not retroactive deletion of D006.

## Source-preservation boundary

The founder does not possess standalone original files for every external-agent
investigation shared during the discovery cycle.

The project MUST NOT fabricate or reconstruct those missing files as if they
were Original Records.

Allowed preservation:

- available prompts/research/simulations that actually exist;
- derived synthesis that clearly labels itself as interpretation;
- hypotheses extracted from the reviewed corpus;
- references noting that external-agent outputs were consulted in conversation.

## Immediate product direction

The strongest current working hypothesis is that these may be complementary
functions, not three competing final products:

- Shared History: historical/context bootstrap;
- Projects/Needs/Opportunities: present action utility;
- Live World: discovery surface;
- Profile: Actor projection.

This is `WORKING HYPOTHESIS`, not a validated product definition.

## Next gate

Prepare `HABITABLE-ALPHA-001` around one real context and one real external
participant.

Primary question:

> Can a person not involved in building Célula Zero enter a small real context,
> find or express a reason to act, involve another person, produce a real-world
> consequence, and later obtain a reason to return?

The experiment must prefer real needs, real people and real actions. Curated is
allowed; fabricated opportunities presented as real are not.

## Authority boundary

This decision authorizes preparation of the product-discovery preservation and
experiment specification.

It does NOT by itself authorize:

- implementation code;
- database/schema changes;
- deployment;
- processing real participant personal data;
- native messaging;
- payment handling;
- blockchain/smart-contract work;
- commit/push/PR/merge unless separately and explicitly authorized for the
  prepared package.

END OF D007
