## Approved Plan

Plan:

`AI-NATIVE COMPANY CORE`

Operational thesis:

> Test whether one legitimate human, using interchangeable AI capabilities, can
> transform real needs into understandable agreements, attributable work,
> useful results and observable economic consequences, repeatedly and across
> multiple projects, without requiring a human team proportional to complexity.

This is a Plan to test, not a validated company claim.

## First habitable product thesis

Working product thesis:

`NEED → CONSEQUENCE`

The first habitable instantiation should allow a real external need to traverse:

`NEED → OPPORTUNITY → CONDITIONS / AGREEMENT → WORK → HUMAN + AI CONTRIBUTIONS → RESULT → RECIPIENT EVALUATION → ECONOMIC CONSEQUENCE → LEARNING / CAPABILITY / CONTEXTUAL TRUST`

It is not selected as:

- a marketplace;
- a universal protocol;
- a universal reputation system;
- a DAO;
- a token economy;
- a blockchain architecture.

A product interface is not required before a real need demonstrates that an
interface preserves a concrete property that manual/existing channels lose.

## Approved roadmap

### P1 — NEED → AGREEMENT

Property:

A real external need can become bounded understandable work with explicit
prospective conditions.

Real test:

One external person/project presents a real need and explicitly accepts the
bounded result, conditions and evaluation criterion.

Does not prove:

useful delivery, economic value, recurrence, PMF, adoption or scale.

### P2 — AGREEMENT → USEFUL RESULT

Property:

Founder + AI capabilities can execute the agreement and deliver a result that
the recipient evaluates under the declared criterion.

AI capabilities are selected by the concrete task.

Multi-AI execution is not a prerequisite.

A second independent AI may be used when there is a concrete hypothesis that
critique, diversity or independent review will improve the result or human
decision surface.

Does not prove:

demand, sustainable economics, recurrence, PMF, adoption or scale.

### P3 — RESULT → ECONOMIC CONSEQUENCE

Property:

A useful result can produce an observable economic consequence while preserving
the distinction between contribution, evaluation, ownership, authority and
economic right.

Possible contextual consequences include, when explicitly defined:

- payment;
- credibly measured time saved;
- avoided cost/loss;
- problem resolved;
- decision enabled;
- opportunity unlocked;
- another agreed observable consequence.

Preserve:

`contribution ≠ payment`

`evaluation ≠ reputation`

`payment ≠ ownership`

`participation ≠ economic right`

Does not prove:

profitability, sustainable business model, PMF, adoption or scale.

### P4 — REPEAT

Property:

The loop can be repeated in another legitimate real need/context rather than
remaining a one-off success.

Repeat only to resolve a new uncertainty.

Does not prove:

large-scale operation or global adoption.

### P5 — MULTI-PROJECT ONE-HUMAN COMPANY

Property:

One legitimate founder can operate two or more real contexts without losing
authority, context, quality, provenance or becoming an unbounded coordination
bottleneck.

Only field pressure at this stage may justify additional orchestration, memory,
routing, specialized AI roles or software.
