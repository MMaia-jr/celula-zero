## Product definition

Célula Zero is an online human–AI institutional environment in which people,
organizations and Cells build presence, transform intention into action and consequence,
compose capabilities from the world, and preserve authority, provenance, learning and
continuity.

The Cell `Célula Zero` is the first organism intended to use Célula Zero to operate,
develop and govern Célula Zero itself.

Preserve:

`CZ COHERENCE ≠ CZ OWNS EVERY COMPONENT`

`EXTERNAL CAPABILITY ≠ EXTERNAL AUTHORITY`

`ARCHITECTURE IS MODULAR ≠ EXPERIENCE IS MODULAR`

`INTERNAL SYSTEM COMPLEXITY ↑ → PERCEIVED HUMAN COMPLEXITY ↓ → HUMAN CAPACITY ↑`

## Human experience direction

The default product experience should expose human intentions rather than internal
ontology or infrastructure.

The main interaction pattern combines:

1. continuity — what makes sense for this person now;
2. clear human actions — create, solve, find, decide, learn, register experience, improve profile;
3. free natural-language input.

The product should not require ordinary users to understand APIs, OAuth, Git, MCP,
webhooks, databases, tokens, repositories, deployment infrastructure, or internal CZ
record types.

Technology appears progressively when a human need requires it.

## Institutional modules

The intended module boundaries are:

1. Identity, Presence & Experience;
2. Cells, Relations & Authority;
3. Records, Knowledge & Provenance;
4. Work & Consequence;
5. Connections & Capabilities;
6. Essenthius & Institutional Memory;
7. Governance;
8. Development & Operations;
9. Economy & Contextual Trust;
10. Portability & Federation.

This is intended as a modular monolith first. Module boundaries do not imply
microservices.

## Huly / platform direction

Huly Core / Platform Collective is an implementation substrate candidate for generic
horizontal capabilities such as collaboration, realtime, documents, files, activity,
notifications, model/plugin runtime and related infrastructure.

Preserve:

`CZ DOMAIN ≠ HULY DOMAIN`

`CZ EXPERIENCE ≠ HULY PRODUCT UI`

`USE HULY WHERE IT REMOVES HORIZONTAL ENGINEERING`

`DO NOT MAKE CZ FIT HULY`

The intended integration classification is:

`ADOPT / MAP / PLUGIN / SERVICE / CORE PATCH / EXTERNAL`

A core patch is justified only when a concrete CZ property cannot be preserved through a
clean extension boundary.

## Connections and capability fabric

External providers are accessed through Connections and exposed institutionally through
a Capability Registry.

A connection must preserve its owner, grant, resources, scopes, credential reference and
status.

Capabilities must preserve, when applicable:

- provider;
- resource;
- read/write character;
- risk;
- cost;
- reversibility;
- required authority;
- approval policy.

All authority-sensitive external actions should pass through an Action Gateway that
separates:

`REQUEST ≠ AUTHORIZATION ≠ EXECUTION ≠ RESULT ≠ CONSEQUENCE`
