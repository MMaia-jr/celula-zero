## Adopted collective dream

Célula Zero should become a living, inhabitable environment in which Humans,
organizations and explicit AI actors can grow together, learn by doing real
work, and transform intention into capability, result, evidence, contextual
trust and opportunity.

Célula Zero is not defined in advance as a SaaS, DAO, Web3 social network,
marketplace, appchain or universal protocol. Some of those forms may appear
where useful; none is the project's identity by itself.

A nontechnical person should be able to enter, understand where they are,
express a Dream or Need, learn, participate, produce, receive or offer help,
form Agreements and possibly earn money without needing Git, terminal,
blockchain or agent architecture literacy.

At the same time, technical people and organizations may use deeper interfaces
such as APIs, automation, agents, self-hosted infrastructure, external identity,
treasuries or smart contracts when concrete needs justify them.

## Adopted principles

1. **Increase autonomy.** Célula Zero should not merely preserve autonomy; it
   should increase Human and Cell autonomy over time. Managed convenience is
   legitimate; dependency as a business strategy is not.

2. **Habitability matters as much as correctness.** A participant should be able
   to experience concrete benefit, increased capability, learning, meaningful
   collaboration, opportunity or income when relevant. Friendship, play,
   dreaming, rest, culture, spiritual practice, disagreement and silence may
   have legitimate space without being forced into productivity or evidence.

3. **Growth is conditional on benefit.**
   `SCALE ≠ SUCCESS BY DEFINITION`
   and
   `SCALE ≠ FAILURE BY DEFINITION`.
   Some Cells may remain intentionally small; others may grow when benefit,
   autonomy, community and quality of relationship survive.

4. **Cells may live, change and end.** Cells may form, grow, transform, split,
   fork, archive or end. Preserve the unresolved tension:
   `MEMORY ↔ FORGETTING`.
   Distinguish:
   `event history ≠ event content ≠ authorship ≠ rights ≠ evidence ≠ retention`.

5. **Trust remains contextual and dissent may persist.** Claims, supporting or
   opposing Evidence, Verifications, dissent and contextual Human Decisions may
   coexist without becoming one universal platform truth.

6. **AI partnership does not confer silent legitimacy.**
   `AI CAPABILITY ≠ AI AUTHORITY`.
   AI authority must be explicit, contextual, bounded and revocable. AI actor
   history should survive provider changes where feasible.

7. **Multiple forms of Human participation are first-class.** Terminal is not
   the hidden "real" interface. Code, voice, physical work, teaching,
   verification, facilitation, funding, introductions, care and learning may
   all be legitimate in context. Computability must not be confused with value.

8. **Economy is real but rights do not appear retroactively by surprise.**
   Célula Zero should become economically sustainable without permanent founder
   subsidy or forced lock-in.
   `PAST ACTIVITY ≠ AUTOMATIC RETROACTIVE ECONOMIC RIGHT`.
   Economic rights should arise from explicit prospective conditions and
   Agreements.

9. **Commons plus legitimate commercial operation.** The Dream favors a
   commons-compatible ecosystem with commercial operations rather than a
   platform whose sustainability requires ownership of participants, Cells,
   identity, authority or data lock-in. The exact commons/commercial boundary
   and license remain undecided.

10. **Web3 remains optional and property-driven.** Wallets, stablecoins,
    multisig, smart contracts, tokens, staking, DAOs, appchains and future
    infrastructure may be used only when a concrete problem justifies them.
    `TECHNOLOGY ≠ IDENTITY`
    `PAYMENT ≠ TOKEN`
    `TOKEN EXISTS ≠ ECONOMIC RIGHT EXISTS`
    `DAO ≠ DEFAULT GOVERNANCE`
    `APPCHAIN ≠ DEFAULT INFRASTRUCTURE`.

11. **Progressive decentralization means optional autonomy.**
    Adopt:
    > **Autonomy should be possible without independence being mandatory.**
    Managed, self-hosted and interoperable Cells may coexist. A Cell may also
    refuse interoperability.
    `OPENNESS ≠ FORCED FEDERATION`.

12. **Genesis may become unnecessary.** Genesis has legitimate founder
    authority today; Célula Zero should not simulate decentralization before
    legitimate collective capacity exists. A mature ecosystem should make it
    possible for Genesis eventually to become unnecessary even if it remains
    active and valuable.

13. **Rootedness without homogenization.** Célula Zero grows from real
    experience in Brazil. International interoperability should not require
    cultural sameness.
    `INTEROPERABILITY ≠ HOMOGENIZATION`.

## Explicitly not adopted as established capability or rule

The Human adoption does **not** establish:

- automatic retroactive economic rights based on "proof of care";
- technical ability to prove that an external model provider erased specific
  information from model weights;
- Cell-internal governance as superior to applicable law or third-party rights;
- poetic metaphors as technical architecture merely because they are compelling;
- AGPL, MPL, Apache or any other final software license;
- a token, DAO, staking system, blockchain or appchain;
- a final business model or pricing structure;
- a final identity architecture;
- a final federation protocol;
- external utility, adoption, PMF or scale.

## Stack-independent constitutional test

Even if Next.js, Supabase, GitHub, OpenAI, Vercel, Safe, blockchain or any other
current technology changes, Célula Zero should remain recognizable if these
properties continue to hold:

- Humans retain real authority and consent;
- AI expands Human capability without silently acquiring legitimacy;
- Cells preserve context and relationships;
- learning happens through real construction;
- observable Results can emerge;
- enough provenance/history exists to understand what happened;
- Claim, Evidence, Verification and Decision remain distinct;
- trust remains contextual;
- economic rights and consequences remain explicit;
- entry, exit and change remain possible;
- the community can transform invisibility into visible capability and real
  opportunity.

## Core adopted proposition

> **Célula Zero should not only preserve autonomy. It should increase autonomy.**

The project should be able to grow without needing to make Humans or Cells
smaller so that the platform becomes larger.
