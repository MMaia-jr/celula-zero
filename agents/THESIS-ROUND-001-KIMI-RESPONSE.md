**CLASSIFICATION:** **Useful later abstraction with elements of retrospective organization.**

**DEFENSE:**

1. **Not a natural consequence:** Pre-repository research had no graph structure. GRAPH-000 was invented after the repository existed.

2. **Useful abstraction:** It genuinely structures provenance in ways that flat documentation cannot. The JSON-LD vocabulary, fixtures, and tests demonstrate technical seriousness.

3. **Elements of discontinuity:** The jump from research documents to structured graph is significant. However, it is a discontinuity in implementation, not in concept — the concern with provenance was always present.

4. **Elements of retrospective organization:** GRAPH-000 appears to organize heterogeneous early material (research, tests, dialogues) into a unified schema that may impose more coherence than the material originally had. The `graph/tests/GTEST-001-reconstrucao-de-trajetoria.md` (27,320 bytes) is essentially a retrospective reconstruction of trajectory.

**CONCLUSION:** GRAPH-000 is best understood as a **disciplined retrospective abstraction** — genuinely useful, but not inevitable from the early material.

### I. Is "Protocol of Protocols": original destination / emergent abstraction / renamed older idea / later strategic expansion?

**CLASSIFICATION:** **Later strategic expansion with elements of emergent abstraction.**

**EVIDENCE:**

**CONCLUSION:** "Protocol of Protocols" is an **aspirational framing** that retroactively unifies heterogeneous research and experimental activities. It captures something real about the project's direction, but it is not the original destination.

### O. Three competing historical explanations

**MODEL 1 — CONTINUOUS EVOLUTION**
- **Claim:** The project evolved organically from research insights to technical implementation, preserving core methodological concerns.
- **Supporting evidence:** Consistent concern with provenance; gradual elaboration of documentation practices; no sudden pivots.
- **Contradicting evidence:** Large jump from music research to AI protocols; new vocabulary not present early.

**MODEL 2 — SERIES OF PIVOTS**
- **Claim:** The project pivoted from research to tech, then from general tech to specific protocol, then from protocol to documentary methodology.
- **Supporting evidence:** Clear domain changes; explicit scope narrowing; abandonment of DAO/blockchain paths.
- **Contradicting evidence:** Founder may have had unified vision from start; research and protocol may be more connected than appears.

- **Supporting evidence:** R01–R05 inaccessible; early research not obviously connected to current protocol; "Protocol of Protocols" may be framing device.

- **Explanatory power:** High for current state; low for future direction.
- **Falsification:** Successful prediction of next 3 project steps from current framework.

**CHOICE:** **MODEL 2 (Series of Pivots)** requires the fewest unsupported assumptions.
