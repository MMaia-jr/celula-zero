## 5. Candidate present-tense thesis

HYPOTHESIS — CANDIDATE THESIS:

Célula Zero is an experimental provenance-and-coordination laboratory
testing a minimal grammar for auditable coordination among heterogeneous
human and artificial agents.

That grammar makes explicit:

- attributable source;
- intention;
- conditions;
- commitment;
- contribution/evidence;
- verification;
- result;
- correction;
- failure.

Its purpose is not to force agents into one internal governance model.

Its research question is whether these minimum interfaces can make
coordination more reconstructable, auditable and interoperable while
preserving distinctions between source, interpretation, authority and
decision.

This wording is a CANDIDATE.

## 11. Product hypothesis

HYPOTHESIS:

The smallest plausible product is not a platform.

It is a portable provenance-and-handoff workflow.

Minimum packet:

1. SOURCE / ACTOR
2. INTENT
3. CONDITIONS
4. COMMITMENT
5. CONTRIBUTION / EVIDENCE
6. VERIFICATION CRITERION
7. RESULT / STATUS
8. UNCERTAINTIES / CORRECTIONS

The packet should allow an uninvolved person or agent to reconstruct:

- who requested what;
- who accepted;
- under which conditions;
- what was delivered;
- which artifact is original;
- how it was verified;
- current status;
- unresolved uncertainty.

No Graph, blockchain, DAO, token or proprietary infrastructure is required
for the first test.

## 14. Candidate external-value experiment

This section is an experiment proposal only.

It does NOT preregister VALUE-TEST-001.

### Participant

One real person who is not the founder and who has an actual collaborative
task involving another human or AI.

### Baseline

Use one comparable task or recent workflow performed in the participant's
ordinary way.

### Treatment

For a new comparable task, use only the minimum provenance-and-handoff packet.

No Graph.

No blockchain.

No custom platform.

### Independent reconstruction

A person or agent who did not participate in execution receives the records
and tries to reconstruct:

1. requester/source;
2. intention;
3. conditions;
4. commitment;
5. delivered contribution;
6. original evidence/artifact;
7. verification method/result;
8. current status/uncertainty.

### Candidate measurements

- reconstruction correctness;
- attribution errors;
- status errors;
- reconstruction time;
- participant recording overhead;
- amount of founder explanation required;
- willingness of participant to reuse the workflow.

### Candidate success signal

At least one external user completes the process on a real task with little
or no founder coaching, and an uninvolved auditor reconstructs the essential
state with fewer material ambiguities than in the user's ordinary workflow,
without recording overhead outweighing the benefit.

Exact thresholds must be preregistered before execution.

### Rejection signal

Reject or materially revise the product hypothesis if:

- the participant needs extensive founder explanation;
- the packet adds more burden than reconstructability benefit;
- attribution/status remain equally ambiguous;
- the participant would not voluntarily reuse it;
- value depends on Célula Zero terminology rather than the underlying mechanism.

## 15. Strategic proposal — adversarial test

### Benefit

A minimal provenance-and-coordination method could improve reconstructability,
handoffs and accountability in human-AI collaborative work without requiring
large infrastructure.

### Hidden assumption

People outside Célula Zero experience provenance/coordination ambiguity as a
real enough problem to justify extra recording discipline.

### Main risk

The project may be formalizing excellent internal research hygiene that does
not create meaningful external value.

### Alternative explanation

Célula Zero may simply be disciplined Git-based documentation plus good
project-management practices expressed in novel vocabulary.

### Cheapest distinguishing test

A real non-founder user performs an actual task using the minimum packet,
followed by independent reconstruction and comparison with ordinary workflow.

### Rejection criterion

If repeated external tests show no material improvement in reconstructability,
attribution, status clarity or handoff quality relative to the added overhead,
the distinctive-method/product thesis should be rejected or narrowed.
