## 2. Method

Only factual premises surviving
THESIS-ROUND-001-FACTUAL-AUDIT
may be treated as factual anchors.

Participant interpretations can still be compared even when they differ.

Repetition receives no automatic epistemic weight.

For each convergence distinguish:

EVIDENCE-DRIVEN

PROMPT-INDUCED

MIXED

A convergence is EVIDENCE-DRIVEN when the underlying observation follows
directly from the frozen corpus regardless of how many participants repeated it.

A convergence is PROMPT-INDUCED when the task explicitly required or strongly
suggested the category of analysis.

A convergence is MIXED when both apply.
