## TASK

Define the minimum product experience for a person who arrives with a broad
dream/intention and should leave with an operational Intent clear enough to
guide one first concrete action.

Do not design the full APP.

## INTENT

Specific Intent for this experiment:

**Projetar uma primeira experiência mínima na qual uma pessoa declare um sonho
e, com assistência humano–IA, saia com uma Intent operacional suficientemente
clara para orientar uma primeira ação concreta, preservando a diferença entre
suas palavras originais e as interpretações produzidas pelo sistema.**

> revolucionar o Mundo, a partir destas ferramentas... quero que seja replicavel

## DECISIONS IN FORCE

1. Git/GitHub is canonical for this experiment.
2. Huly is an operational projection, not canonical.
3. A2A is an experimental handoff envelope, not proof of direct interoperability
   between current chat interfaces.

5. Product may propose; it may not silently alter the Intent.
6. Original Record must remain separate from Interpretation.
7. The first action should be small, concrete and reversible.
8. The experiment does not build the full APP.
9. No new custom integration is required merely to complete this slice.
10. No agent consensus constitutes legitimacy or human decision.

- Do not require the user to understand Git, Huly, A2A or internal architecture.
- No mandatory wallet.

- Do not present an AI interpretation as the user's original statement.
